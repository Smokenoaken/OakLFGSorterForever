# v0.1.0

Initial Forever release.

- Compact Oak browser for shared player and group listings.
- Sortable columns, activity selection, role filters, and text search.
- Group tooltips show member names, classes, levels, and advertised roles.
- Whisper drafts and native solo-player invites.
- Access to Blizzard's listing editor and browser.
- Draggable Oak minimap button with saved position.
- Open / Close Sorter keybinding in the Oak LFG Sorter - Forever category.
- Saved window position, scale options, and optional auto-open.
